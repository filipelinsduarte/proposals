02Search trend

August gave back part of July’s record, but the cause is narrow

July was an all-time high at 1,409 clicks. August is tracking at 981 through the 26th. The decline sits almost entirely in one page and one head term, while brand demand and comparison content both grew.

Still well ahead year on year

981 clicks in 26 days versus 395 for all of Nov 2025. The trajectory is intact.

Impressions fell further than clicks

Down 11.6% with position slipping to 13.6, so this is ranking loss, not demand loss.

Why organic matters more than its share suggests

Organic produced 22% of pipeline but 34% of closed revenue, because those buyers convert far more reliably. Its average completed order is $703 against $645 for Direct. Growing organic pipeline is therefore worth more per dollar than growing any other channel, which is what makes the Buy Tradelines ranking recovery the priority for September.

The ChatGPT order is worth understanding

A single order at $1,949, cancelled before payment. One data point proves nothing about the channel, but it was the largest attributed order of the month at 5.6x the median order value. AI assistants also show separately in GA4 at 19 sessions. Worth finding out why it did not complete.

What is consuming the crawl budget

204 of 309 indexable pages are individual product listings with titles such as 2018 Dec and No Age, the latter repeated 16 times. Google has already refused to index 58 of them. Noindexing the set would formalise a decision Google has made on its own and return that crawl budget to the pages that can rank.

Already addressed this month

Three articles in the not indexed list were buried at crawl depth 4 and 5. The new category structure and internal linking moved every one of them to depth 2. That change needs two to four weeks of recrawl before it shows in Search Console.

Why brokers are the right persona

A buyer can be pre-qualified on income and down payment and still stall at underwriting because the file is thin or utilization is high against limits. An authorized user tradeline puts an aged account and its credit limit on the report, which changes the file age and utilization mix underwriters read.

Mortgage brokers and F&I managers both see the credit pull before the deal is structured, so they are the two roles positioned to raise the option early enough to matter. Tradelines take days to post, which makes that timing the whole point.

One broker is a proof of concept rather than a revenue line. A single referring partner can send repeat clients, so the measure to watch is orders attributed to the programme, not sign ups.
