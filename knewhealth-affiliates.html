Common questions

Questions affiliates ask before applying.

How much does the Knew Health affiliate program pay?

The published commission is $500 per referred member. Commissions are paid monthly, and your affiliate portal gives you daily tracking reports so you can see clicks and referrals as they come in. There are no tiers to unlock and no volume threshold before a referral counts.

How long does the affiliate tracking cookie last?

Cookie tracking runs for 365 days. If someone clicks your tracking link and then joins Knew Health within that window, the referral is still attributed to you. Tracking is applied automatically to the direct links you create from your website.

Is the affiliate program the same as the member referral program?

No, they are two separate programs. The affiliate program is for creators, publishers and people with a health and wellness audience, and it pays a $500 commission through an affiliate portal. The member referral program is for people who are already Knew Health members, and it pays a $300 Ambassador Bonus as a credit toward future membership contributions after the referred membership has been active for 90 days.

How do I become a Knew Health affiliate?

There are three steps. First, fill out the application on the Knew Health affiliate portal. Second, our team reviews your application. Third, once you are approved you will receive an email invite to create your account and collect your tracking links.

Do I need to be a Knew Health member to become an affiliate?

The published criteria are having a large email or social media following, or a website or blog focused on health and wellness. Every application is reviewed by the Knew Health team before approval, so the fit between your audience and the Community matters more than anything else.

Are there states where Knew Health does not pay referral fees?

Yes. Knew Health cannot and does not pay referral fees for the referral of new members in Pennsylvania and Maryland. Knew Health is also not accepting new members in Washington, Vermont, Illinois, New York, Pennsylvania, Maryland, California or Oregon. The state availability page is the current source of truth, so check it before promoting to a specific state.

Can affiliates describe Knew Health as health insurance?

No. Knew Health is not an insurance company and the membership is not offered through an insurance company. Affiliates must never represent the membership as insurance, a health plan, coverage, or a substitute for either, and must not describe themselves as an agent, broker, producer, or licensed representative. Use membership, monthly contribution, IUA, Need, and medical cost sharing instead.

Does Knew Health review affiliate marketing content?

Yes. Written communications require approval from Knew Health before you publish them. Send drafts to affiliate@knewhealth.com. Our team can also assist with your email copy, site copy and social media if you would like the support, which is usually faster than writing it and then reworking it after review.
