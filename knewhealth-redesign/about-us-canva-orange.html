About us

A more human approach to healthcare.

Healthcare can feel expensive, confusing and strangely impersonal. Knew Health was built to work the other way round: a community that shares the cost when something big happens, and pays for the everyday care that keeps it from happening.

Why we exist

You spend more every year and get less back.

Three things shaped how this community works, and they are the same three things members tell us they were looking for.

The problem

Premiums climb every renewal, deductibles climb with them, and most years you still pay for nearly all of your own care.

What we do instead

Members pay a flat monthly amount, and the community shares eligible costs under guidelines you read before joining.

Where it came from

Founded in 2017 by Joshua Rosenthal and a group who wanted affordability, choice and transparency to arrive in one product.
