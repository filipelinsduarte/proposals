The process

How a Knew Health membership works, step by step.

The real worry is whether anyone shows up when something goes wrong. Your monthly contribution and your Initial Unshareable Amount decide that.

1

Join in any month

There is no enrollment window. Apply online, answer a short set of health questions, and start on the first of any month.

2

Contribute monthly

A flat monthly amount set by your age, household size and the IUA you choose. It has not gone up for any member since 2018.

3

Choose your provider

There is no network to stay inside. See any licensed provider you like, tell them you are self-pay, and ask for the bill.

4

Submit your Need

Send the itemized bill through your member portal. Our Care Team reviews it with you against the Membership Guidelines.

5

The community shares

You take care of your IUA on that Need. Eligible amounts above it are shared, typically within 7 to 14 days of review.

The one bit of jargon

Your Initial Unshareable Amount, in plain English.

This is the one piece of jargon worth learning, because it sets both what you pay each month and what you pay when something actually happens.

$1,000 IUA

Highest monthly contribution, smallest amount on any single Need.

$2,500 IUA

A middle option that balances contribution against what you handle.

$5,000 IUA

Lowest monthly contribution, in exchange for handling more per Need.

A worked example. Say you choose the $2,500 IUA and have a $9,400 hospital bill. You take care of the first $2,500 of that Need, and the remaining $6,900 is submitted to the Community for sharing. Eligible amounts are typically shared within 7 to 14 days. Your IUA applies to each Need on its own rather than building up across a calendar year, so there is nothing to reach before an everyday visit, and a quiet year does not reset anything.
What is shareable

What the Community shares in, and where the limits sit.

You should know the limits before you join, not while you are sitting in a waiting room. The Membership Guidelines are the final word on both sides.

Commonly shareable

  • Hospital stays, surgery and emergency care
  • Doctor visits, labs and diagnostic imaging
  • Telemedicine, around the clock, at no extra cost
  • Preventive bloodwork and one-to-one health coaching
  • An annual wellness allowance you direct yourself

Limits worth knowing

  • Sharing for pre-existing conditions phases in
  • Some services are shared only after a waiting time
  • Elective and cosmetic procedures are not shared
  • Sharing amounts follow the Membership Guidelines
  • Nothing here is a legal promise to pay a bill
The guidelines are the real answer. This page is a summary written to be readable, not the governing document. The Knew Health Membership Guidelines set out exactly what is eligible for sharing, what the timelines are, and how each category is treated. Read them before you join, and call the Care Team on 855-542-0050 if anything in them is unclear. Knew Health does not turn applicants away because of past medical conditions, and the sharing timelines for those conditions are written out in full there.

Common questions

The questions people ask us before they join.

These come up most often on calls with our Care Team, and they are the ones people tell us are hardest to get a straight answer to.

What is medical cost sharing?

Medical cost sharing is a membership model. Members pay a monthly contribution into a community, and when someone has a large medical expense, the Community shares in the eligible part of it. Knew Health is not an insurance company and the membership is not insurance. What you get instead is a defined process, published Membership Guidelines and a Care Team who walks each Need through with you.

Is Knew Health the same as health insurance?

No. Knew Health is not a traditional health insurance plan and the membership is not insurance. It does not satisfy state or federal requirements for minimum essential coverage, and no member is compelled by law to contribute toward anyone else's medical bills. Many people arrive here comparing health insurance alternatives, so the honest framing is a different mechanism with different trade-offs.

What is an Initial Unshareable Amount (IUA)?

Your IUA is the amount you take care of on a single Need before the Community shares in the rest of it. Knew Health offers three: $1,000, $2,500 and $5,000. A lower IUA comes with a higher monthly contribution, and a higher IUA comes with a lower one. The IUA applies to each Need on its own rather than building up across a calendar year, which members often tell us they had misunderstood.

How long does it take for a Need to be shared?

Eligible medical expenses are typically shared within 7 to 14 days once the Need has been submitted and reviewed, which is faster than is common in this category. The timing depends on how quickly the itemized bill reaches us from the provider and whether the Care Team needs anything further from you, so submitting the bill promptly is the single biggest thing you can do to keep it moving.

Can I keep my own doctor?

Yes. There are no network restrictions, so you can see any licensed provider you choose, including the one you see today. You present yourself as a self-pay patient, which on its own often reduces the bill before it is ever submitted, and the Care Team works on the amount with the provider where doing so helps. Nothing on this page requires you to change doctor, hospital or clinic.

Do I need to be religious to join?

No. Knew Health is secular. There is no statement of faith, no church attendance requirement and no lifestyle covenant, and Knew Health does not operate as a health care sharing ministry. A large part of this category is faith-based by design, which is why the question comes up on almost every call, and the difference here is a real one rather than a matter of emphasis.

When can I join, and what about pre-existing conditions?

You can join in any month. There is no annual window to wait for, and memberships begin on the first of the month after you apply. Knew Health does not turn applicants away because of past medical conditions, though sharing for pre-existing conditions phases in over time rather than starting on day one. The Membership Guidelines set out exactly how that works, and reading them before you apply is time well spent.
