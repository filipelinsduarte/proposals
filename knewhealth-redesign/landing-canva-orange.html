A sharing community, not an insurance policy. A healthcare sharing community should not be viewed as an insurance policy, either group or individual, as it operates on principles of voluntary financial assistance among its members. It is crucial to recognize these distinctions when considering participation in such a community. Knew Health is not a traditional health insurance plan and the membership is not insurance. It does not satisfy state or federal requirements for minimum essential coverage, no member is compelled by law to contribute toward anyone else’s medical bills, and you remain personally responsible for your own bills. What the membership offers is a defined, published process for handling them, not a legal promise of payment. The full notice is at the foot of this page and is worth reading in full.

Before you decide

The questions members ask us first.

Is this health insurance?

No. Knew Health is not a traditional health insurance plan and the membership is not insurance. Members contribute monthly into a community that shares one another’s eligible medical costs. It does not satisfy state or federal requirements for minimum essential coverage, and no member is compelled by law to contribute toward your bills. What you get is a defined, published process, not a legal promise of payment.

Can I keep my own doctor?

Yes. There is no network. You see any licensed provider you like, tell them you are self-pay, and ask for the bill. Self-pay pricing is often lower than the rate an insurer would have been billed.

What happens when a big bill lands?

You submit it as a Need. You pay your Initial Unshareable Amount, which you chose at $1,000, $2,500 or $5,000 and which applies per Need rather than building up over a year. Once it is met, the community shares the eligible remainder and Knew Health pays the provider directly rather than reimbursing you.

Do I have to affirm a statement of faith?

No. Three of the seven healthshare communities we compare require one. Knew Health does not.

What about a condition I already have?

Pre-existing conditions phase in over time rather than being ruled out permanently. The published Membership Guidelines are the governing document and set out the schedule, and the Care Team will walk you through how yours would be treated before you join.
